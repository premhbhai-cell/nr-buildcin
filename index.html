FAQ

Questions before you start?

Quick answers to what clients ask us most.

How do I get a quote?

Call 8080888577, message us on WhatsApp or send the enquiry form. Tell us the type of building, location, approximate size and budget, and we will explain the next steps.

Do you take new construction and renovation work?

Yes. We build new houses, commercial buildings and multi-storey structures, and also handle renovation, remodelling and interior finishing.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based at Post Koradi, Nagpur and serve clients across Nagpur. Call us with your site location and we will confirm.

Can you help with planning before construction?

Yes. We begin with a consultation and site survey, agree scope and an itemised estimate, and coordinate drawings before construction starts.

Do I need drawings before contacting you?

No. If you already have plans we can build from them. If not, we can help coordinate with an architect or engineer.

How will I know how the work is progressing?

We agree the schedule at the start and keep you updated through the work, so you always know where the project stands.
